- Every user query is processed by Gemini 2.5 Flash autonomously
- AI generates expert tax guidance with section numbers and deadlines
- No human intervention in any response
- All interactions permanently logged in Google Firestore at /logs endpoint
- AI handles GST, ITR, TDS, compliance queries in Hindi & English
